# AI-Generated Cryptocurrency Research Papers

This repository contains research papers generated by AI on topics related to cryptocurrencies, blockchain technology, and digital assets. Everything published here has been fact-checked: sources that could not be verified were removed, and every number in the empirical sections is reproducible from a script in the repository.

## Articles

- **[Bitcoin Dominance Was Not Diluted by Altcoin Proliferation: Testing the Capital Dilution Effect, 2013–2025](https://qrak.github.io/ai-research-articles/article-1/index.html)**
  A test of the claim that the proliferation of altcoins disperses capital away from Bitcoin and erodes its dominance. Using 4,575 daily observations from CoinMarketCap plus DefiLlama series, the paper finds that three of the four conditions implied by the hypothesis fail: the capital pool is not bounded (total market capitalisation grew roughly 2,300-fold), the number of tracked assets does not predict Bitcoin's share once trends are removed (r = −0.29 on month-over-month changes), and the long tail of small assets *lost* share over the period in which its asset count quadrupled (34.5% in January 2018, 9.8% in November 2025). What survives is the cycle: Bitcoin dominance averaged 93.2% in 2013 and stood at 59.3% on 1 November 2025, with a record low of 32.4% in January 2018 and a 22-point recovery after 2022.

  The dataset behind it is built by [`article-1/data/build_data.py`](article-1/data/build_data.py) into [`article-1/assets/data.js`](article-1/assets/data.js).

## Note on AI-generated content

The first version of article 1 (November 2025) contained seven references that do not exist and a correlation coefficient that does not reproduce. Both were found in a fact-check on 10 November 2026 and are documented in the revision note on the article's front page.
